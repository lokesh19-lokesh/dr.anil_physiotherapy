Non-Emergency Healthcare Notice

Dr. Anil's Physiotherapy and Pain Relief Clinic is an outpatient rehabilitation and musculoskeletal facility. If you or someone you know is experiencing acute emergency symptoms (such as sudden chest pressure, loss of consciousness, uncontrolled bleeding, acute neurological deficits, or severe bone fracture), please visit the nearest emergency medical hospital immediately (such as Saiteja Hospital situated beside our clinic).
