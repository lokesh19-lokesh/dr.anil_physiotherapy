FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Helpful information to prepare you for your physiotherapy consultation in Miyapur.

Physiotherapy addresses a wide variety of musculoskeletal conditions including acute and chronic back pain, cervical neck stiffness, sports injuries (sprains, strains), frozen shoulder, post-surgical recovery (such as knee or hip arthroplasty), postural dysfunction, and joint stiffness.

In general, direct consultations do not require a prior physician referral. If you already possess medical reports, scans, or post-surgical protocols from an orthopedic surgeon, we encourage you to bring them along for your initial assessment.

Initial evaluations typically span 45 to 60 minutes, allowing adequate time for history taking, functional movement tests, and initial gentle therapy. Subsequent treatment sessions usually range between 30 and 45 minutes depending on your individual protocol.

Recovery duration varies according to condition severity, tissue healing timelines, and individual compliance with home exercises. We formulate clear, milestone-based treatment plans and regularly reassess progress rather than prescribing arbitrary session packages.
